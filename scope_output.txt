AI E-commerce Optimization Platform
Demo MVP Scope
1. Demo Objective
Build a lightweight AI-powered e-commerce optimization platform that demonstrates:
Shopify data → AI analysis → actionable business recommendations
The demo should make a potential client understand the product within 5–10 minutes.
2. Demo Flow
The complete demo flow should be:
Login
  ↓
Connect / Select Shopify Store
  ↓
Dashboard
  ↓
AI analyzes store data
  ↓
AI Recommendations
  ↓
Recommendation Details
  ↓
Suggested Action
For the demo, Shopify data can be real or mocked, depending on development time.

Support a demo store with realistic data.

This ensures the demo is reliable even without depending on a live Shopify account.

7. Recommendation Detail
Clicking a recommendation opens a detail page/modal.
Keep it simple:
Product X — Pricing Opportunity
Recommendation
Increase price by 5%
Why?
Sales have increased 14% while demand remains
strong and discounting has decreased.
Supporting Data
Revenue       +18%
Units Sold    +11%
Discounts      -8%
AI Suggested Action
Test a 5% price increase.
[Mark as Done]    [Dismiss]
The important part is showing why the AI made the recommendation.
8. AI Chat — Demo Feature
Include a small AI chat panel if time permits.
Example questions:

Why are sales declining?
Who should I target with a campaign?
What should I do this week?
The AI responds using the demo store's dataset.
